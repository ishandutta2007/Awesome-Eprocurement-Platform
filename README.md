# Awesome-Eprocurement-Platform

## Top Event Registration Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Event Ticketing, Registration Forms, Attendee Management, Check-in, Payments & Event Websites*

**Last updated: August 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Event Registration**. These tools handle online registration, ticketing, payments, custom forms, attendee management, check-in (including QR), event websites, and basic marketing for conferences, workshops, festivals, corporate events, and community gatherings.



**Examples** include Cvent, Eventbrite, Bizzabo, Swoogo, Whova, Hopin Events (RingCentral Events), Splash, Ticket Tailor, Accelevents, and RegFox (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, zero per-ticket fees, full branding control, and data ownership — ideal for organizers, venues, communities, and developers who want transparent, customizable alternatives to proprietary ticketing platforms.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Cvent](https://www.cvent.com/)**  

  Enterprise event management and registration platform with advanced forms, multi-session support, venue sourcing, governance, onsite tools, and deep analytics for large conferences and complex programs.

- **[Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/)**  

  Popular self-service ticketing and discovery platform for public events, workshops, and community gatherings. Strong marketplace visibility, easy setup, and integrated payments (with per-ticket fees).

- **[Bizzabo](https://www.bizzabo.com/)**  

  Connected event experience platform focused on B2B conferences, hybrid events, attendee engagement, smart badges, sponsor ROI, and modern registration experiences.

- **[Swoogo](https://swoogo.events/)**  

  Highly customizable registration and event website platform popular with mid-market and agency teams. Strong form flexibility, unlimited events options, and fast setup.

- **[Whova](https://whova.com/)**  

  Event platform emphasizing attendee networking, mobile apps, community engagement, agendas, and onsite tools — frequently used by associations and academic conferences.

- **[Hopin Events](https://hopin.com/)** (RingCentral Events)  

  Virtual and hybrid event platform with registration, virtual venues, networking, and webinar-style capabilities.

- **[Splash](https://splashthat.com/)** (now part of Cvent ecosystem)  

  Marketing-led event platform known for beautiful branded landing pages, registration, and field/marketing event workflows.

- **[Ticket Tailor](https://www.tickettailor.com/)**  

  Flexible, fee-friendly ticketing platform focused on simplicity, customization, and lower costs for organizers who want more control than marketplace-heavy tools.

- **[Accelevents](https://www.accelevents.com/)**  

  All-in-one platform supporting virtual, hybrid, and in-person events with registration, engagement, and streaming features.

- **[RegFox](https://www.regfox.com/)**  

  Registration and ticketing solution often used for races, festivals, and complex multi-ticket events with strong form and payment capabilities.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[pretix](https://github.com/pretix/pretix)**  

  Mature, full-featured open-source ticket shop (AGPLv3) for conferences, festivals, concerts, workshops, and exhibitions. Supports complex pricing, seating plans, check-in apps, plugins, and both self-hosted and hosted options. One of the strongest production-ready alternatives to commercial ticketing platforms.

- **[Hi.Events](https://github.com/HiEventsDev/Hi.Events)**  

  Modern open-source event management and ticket-selling platform. Flexible ticket types, promo codes, capacity management, custom branding, PDF tickets, embeddable widgets, and full self-hosting with no per-ticket fees.

- **[Attendize](https://github.com/Attendize/Attendize)**  

  Open-source ticket selling and event management platform built on Laravel. Includes event pages, attendee management, QR tickets, statistics, multi-organizer support, and self-hosting without third-party service fees.

- **[Indico](https://github.com/indico/indico)**  

  Powerful open-source event management system (originally from CERN) focused on conferences, meetings, and multi-day events. Strong in abstract/CfP handling, scheduling, registration, and large academic or scientific events.

- **[Open Event / related FOSS event platforms](https://github.com/fossasia)** (and community forks)  

  Open-source event solutions covering registration, scheduling, and related features, often used in FOSS and community contexts.

- **[OSEM](https://github.com/openSUSE/osem)**  

  Open Source Event Manager tailored for free and open-source software conferences, with registration, scheduling, and speaker management.

- **[pretalx](https://github.com/pretalx/pretalx)**  

  Conference planning tool focused on Call for Papers (CfP), scheduling, and speaker management — often paired with ticketing systems.

- **[alf.io](https://github.com/alfio-event/alf.io)**  

  Open-source event ticketing and check-in platform with QR support, suitable for conferences, trade shows, and workshops.

- **[gath.io](https://github.com/)** and similar lightweight RSVP tools  

  Simple open-source event planning and RSVP systems for smaller or free events.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **White-label / multi-tenant platforms** such as Event Schedule and similar self-hostable ticketing SaaS starters.

- **QR-based and offline check-in systems** built with modern stacks (Next.js, Laravel, etc.).

- **Conference-focused tools**: combinations of Indico + pretix, or OSEM + custom registration.

- Community **form builders, payment integrations (Stripe, etc.), badge printers, and attendee apps**.

- Many smaller **Laravel/PHP, Python/Django, and Node.js event/ticketing starters** available on GitHub.

- Supporting tools for **seating charts, waitlists, promo codes, and post-event surveys**.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Use **pretix** or **Hi.Events** / **Attendize** as the core ticketing engine, pair with **Indico** or **pretalx** for complex conference programming, add open-source form builders and payment gateways, and deploy on your own infrastructure for full branding and data ownership with zero platform per-ticket fees.
